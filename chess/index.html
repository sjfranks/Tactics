Dice

2d6 roll when a piece strikes.

Log

    New game

    Each side places its pieces on its back two rows. With a king, kill the enemy king to win; without one, wipe out the enemy army.

    Pass to Black

    Each side sets up without seeing the other's pieces. Both armies appear when the battle begins.

    Rules

    Setup. Each side places its pieces on its own back two rows and sets each piece's facing (the small arrow). White sets up first, then Black.

    Your turn. Each of your pieces may move and take one action. A piece moves up to 3 squares orthogonally (no diagonals). It may pass through friends but not foes, and it ends on an empty square. You can take a move back until that piece rolls dice.

    Facing. When you end your turn, you set the facing of each of your pieces, then the other side plays. The side of a foe you strike matters: its front is the square it faces, its back is the square behind, and its flanks are the two sides.

    Moving. On your turn the next piece that can act is already selected (gold outline). Tap a blue square to move it, or tap another of your pieces to pick that one instead.

    Striking. Tap a foe in red. The piece walks to the best square next to it, and 2d6 are rolled. Meet or beat the number to hit.

    Grapple (pawns): on 8+, you push or pull the foe one square orthogonally onto any empty square next to it. You can use it on a foe from any side, even one that faces you, to turn a fight around.

    Winning. Kill the enemy king. If the armies have no kings, kill every enemy piece.

    Keys: Enter ends your turn or confirms facing. Esc deselects. When setting facing, the arrow keys turn the selected piece. A and G switch between Attack and Grapple.
