    How to play

    Setup

    Each side places its army on its own back two rows (the ember squares). Pick a piece on the hotbar, then tap a square. Tap a placed piece to pick it up, then tap a square to move it. To turn a piece, swipe from it the way it should face, or tap one of the arrows round it.

    Your turn: one piece

    • White and Black take turns. Each turn you use one piece: it may move and take one action. A piece is picked for you; tap another to use it instead.
    • Move: up to 3 squares, never diagonally. Tap a pale square, or drag the piece there. Undo takes the move back (and frees you to pick another piece) until it rolls dice.
    • Strike: choose Attack or Grapple on the hotbar. Foes you can reach are marked red with the odds over their heads. Tap one: the path lights up with Confirm?. Tap it again to walk there and roll 2d6.
    • Then set the facing of the piece you used and tap Done. Only that piece turns; the rest hold still. (Tap End to stop early or to pass.)

    Facing

    The arrow on the ground under each piece is the way it faces. Its front is the square ahead, its back the square behind, its flanks the two sides. Strikes to the back hurt most, and a pawn cannot attack a foe's front at all.

    Winning

    Kill the enemy king. Without kings, kill every enemy piece.

    Keys: 1–5 pick from the hotbar, Enter ends the turn, Esc cancels, arrow keys turn the selected piece while setting facing.
