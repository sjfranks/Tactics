How to play

Each turn both managers give an order to each of their four players at the same time. Nobody sees the other side's orders until both have locked in. Then everything happens together: first everyone moves, then the ball moves, then tackles land. The match is 16 turns, two halves of 8. A draw goes to a penalty shoot-out.

The squad

  • Keeper: also your last defender. Only a keeper can Dive and catch a cross in their own box. A keeper holding the ball in their own box can't be tackled.
  • Two midfielders: pass up to 5 squares, everyone else 4.
  • Striker: power shots. From 4 rows out, anyone else's shot is an easy save for a keeper standing near that post.
  • Everyone runs up to 2 squares a turn in any direction, diagonals too.

With the ball

  • Dribble up to 2 squares. A tackle aimed where you were finds nothing.
  • Pass along a straight line on the ground. Any opponent standing on that line once the moves are done cuts it out. Pass to an empty square and a teammate who runs there takes it: a through ball.
  • Shoot at the left or right side of the goal from their half. Anyone on the line blocks it; the keeper saves it by standing in its way or diving to that side.
  • Chip from 2 to 4 rows out floats over everyone. It goes in unless their keeper is on the goal line near it, or a defender is standing on the line at that post.
  • Shield: stand firm. Beats a standing tackle, but not a slide.
  • Each player gets one order a turn: a player who passes or shoots stands still to do it, after everyone else has moved.

Combos you lock in together

  • Cross → Header: from a wide channel (the two outer columns each side) in their half, cross to a square in their box. The ball flies over everyone. Give a teammate Header on that same square and they head it at goal the moment it lands. Whoever stands on the square gets there first: a defender clears it, their keeper catches it.
  • Through ball: pass to the square a teammate is running to.
  • One-two: pass, and next turn have it played back to the passer.

Without the ball

  • Block by standing on the line of a pass or shot. This is most of defending: guess where the ball goes and be there.
  • Tackle a square next to you. Once the ball has moved, whoever has it on that square loses it: the carrier, or the player a pass just found. Only a Shield stops it.
  • Slide 2 squares in a line, once the ball has moved. Takes it from whoever has it anywhere on that line, shield or not. Miss and you are down next turn. Hit a player without the ball and it's a foul: a free kick, or a penalty in your own box.
  • Dive (keeper on their goal line): commit to one side. Any shot or header to that side is saved.
  • Two players running for the same square bump and both stop short. A loose ball belongs to whoever reaches it.

Reading the other side

  • Shield beats tackle, slide beats shield, dribbling away beats both. Diving the right way beats a shot; staying on your line beats a chip.
  • Their last orders (in the ☰ menu) shows what the other side did last turn. The goblins also remember which way you shoot and dive.

Controls

  • Tap one of your players (or their badge at the top), pick an order on the hotbar, then tap a square. For shots, chips and dives tap the left or right half of the goal. Header takes two taps: the square, then a side of the goal.
  • Players without an order hold. Lock when you're done.
