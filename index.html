Tactics 48

Activation

Choose any ready hero. Take one Move and one Action in either order. Powers can add extra movement.

No whiffs

Roll 2d6 + Might, Agility or Will against Defense. Every attack produces a Tier 1, 2 or 3 result.

Position

Flank from opposite sides for +1. Leaving engagement costs +1 Move. Cover adds +1 Defense against ranged attacks.

Momentum

Earn it from flanks, defeating enemies, objectives and terrain plays. Spend the shared pool on Heroic powers.

Terrain

Walls block sight. Difficult ground costs +1. High ground adds 1 range. Fire Burns. Forced movement into a wall deals +2.

Downed

Heroes at 0 HP are Downed, not dead. Mira can Restore them; any adjacent hero can Rally them.
