Goblin Crypt

Five battles stand between your party and the Goblin King: a gorge crossing, a ridge camp, the warg pens, the forge pits and his throne room. Every hero has 3 actions on their turn, every goblin 2. Moving is an action, and so is each ability. The dice are real dice: a 1 misses, a top face is a crit that explodes into another die, and the target's armour takes a fixed amount off the rest. The party rests to full health between battles.

The campaign

  • After each victory the party makes camp and picks one of three boons: a better weapon, tougher armour, more health, a faster cooldown, Healing Draughts for everyone. Boons last the rest of the campaign; each hero's sheet lists theirs.
  • Experience: in the campaign each hero earns a little XP for every action: 6 for a hit, 14 more for each foe slain, 6 for a heal, 4 for a helping spell, and 20 for still standing at a victory. 100 XP is a level, and each level adds 2 max HP. Levels 2, 3 and 4 each teach a new power: Brakka learns Shield Bash, Execute and War Cry; Sister Wenna Smite, Ward and Prayer of Mending; Orlen Chain Lightning, Mana Shield and Blink; Vex Throwing Knives, Poisoned Blade and Smoke Bomb. The hero's sheet shows how close the next level is.
  • Battle close-ups: when an attack, a spell or a heal is locked in, its dice are thrown first; then the camera swings in to show the blow as it fell, and goes back. Settings turns them off; tapping during the goblins' turn skips them.
  • Healing Draught: one a battle. Drink it, or pour it for a friend beside you, to heal 2d4.
  • The campaign saves itself in this browser after every move. Close the page and come back to the same turn.
  • Settings has the difficulty (goblins have 75%, 100% or 125% of their health; it counts from the next battle), game speed, music, banter, and a new campaign.

Controls

  • Tap a blue square or drag a hero to move.
  • Pick an ability on the hotbar (or press its number), then tap a target. Each target shows the odds over its head: the damage to expect and the chance to finish it (KO). If you'd have to walk there first, the path lights up with Confirm?: tap it (or the target again) to go, anywhere else to cancel.
  • Red arrows show what each goblin means to do if it acted now: who it would strike, or where it would walk. The bold one acts next. They change as you play, so use them: step out of reach, hide, or Taunt and watch every arrow swing to Brakka.
  • Undo in the ☰ menu takes back moves and abilities that rolled no dice. Dice are final: once a roll is made it stands, and so does everything before it.
  • Twist two fingers to turn the board, pinch to zoom, and drag the floor to look around. On a computer, shift-drag or right-drag turns it. Tap during the goblins' turn to speed it up.
  • Each hero has five hotbars of five slots: page with the arrows beside the bar, a sideways swipe, or [ and ].
  • Keys: 1–5 pick an ability, E or Enter ends the turn, Z undoes, Esc puts the ability down, F (or Page Up and Page Down) switches floors in a house. In a scene, Enter or a tap goes on and Esc skips.

Turns

  • At the start of the battle every piece rolls initiative once (d10, plus 3 for Vex and wargs, 1 for Orlen, cutters and archers, −1 for Grubnash) and keeps it to the end; a goblin that wakes or is called in rolls when it joins. The higher roll goes first, then heroes and goblins alternate, one piece at a time. The strip at the top shows who acts now and who comes next.
  • Disadvantage: each attack after a piece's first in a turn throws one more die for every attack it has already made, and the highest are set aside.
  • A hero at 0 HP is down, not dead: they lie where they fell, and goblins leave the body be. Sister Wenna's Heal raises them with whatever it rolls. If every hero is down, the battle is lost.
  • A Prone hero spends an action to stand up.

Attack and armour

  • The attacker rolls its dice. If every die it keeps is a 1, the attack misses, and nothing that rides on the hit happens.
  • Otherwise the target takes the total less its armour: the number on the shield beside its health bar. There is no roll for it.
  • A crit (a die on its top face) cuts straight through armour. Only blows up close crit: arrows, bolts, rays, hexes and Fireball never do.
  • Armour: Brakka 2, Sister Wenna and Vex 1, Orlen none; cutters and archers none, shamans 1, wargs 2, Grubnash 3.
  • Taunt adds 1, Prone takes 1 off, Rebuke takes 2 off until Wenna's next turn, and Frozen leaves none. The shield turns red while armour is stripped.
  • One roll, many targets: Cleave, Thunderwave and Fireball roll once, and each figure caught loses its own armour. Grubnash Smash, fire, falls and slams ignore armour.

Combos

  • Prone and Frozen foes take +1 die from every hit and lose armour. Charge, Thunderwave and Dirty Trick knock them down; Frost Ray freezes.
  • Rebuke then strike: 2 off a foe's armour, so a warg's is gone and Grubnash's falls to 1.
  • Charge a goblin into a wall, a pillar or another piece for a +2 Slam; shove it into fire for +2 burn.
  • Grapple moves a foe or a friend beside Brakka one square in any direction: drag a goblin off a ledge or into the gorge, or pull a friend out of harm's way.
  • Hide lasts until the end of Vex's next turn. Strike from hiding for +2 dice; add Bless and a prone target for a five-die backstab.
  • Haste gives a hero a fourth action on their next turn.
  • Taunt pulls every goblin onto Brakka, his armour 1 higher, while the others work.
  • Fireball leaves burning squares for 2 rounds; anything standing in fire takes 2 each turn. Fire burns friends too.

Terrain

  • High ground: a ranged attack on a foe at least a step lower adds +1 die. Melee gets no height bonus.
  • Cliffs up to 2 squares high can be climbed, up or down; each square of height costs an extra square of movement. Chevrons mark a square you'd reach by climbing.
  • Engaged: a shooter or caster (mage, archer, shaman) with a foe right beside it can't attack or cast unless it stands higher. Stepping away gives each such foe a free strike.
  • Cover: a ranged attack on a target with a wall, rock, tree, pillar or crate right beside it, on the shooter's side, rolls with disadvantage.
  • Ice Wall raises three blocks in a row (⟲ turns it across or down). They block movement and sight and give cover; a basic attack breaks one, and they melt after 3 rounds. Ice raised on fire turns to steam and puts it out; Fireball melts ice; Frost Ray can't freeze a foe standing in fire.
  • Ledges: a foe shoved or grappled off high ground takes +2. Gorges can only be crossed on the bridges, and a goblin thrown in falls to its death.
  • Lava can't be entered. A goblin shoved, charged or grappled into it takes +5 and is knocked Prone. Fire vents erupt every second round and throb the round before: anyone standing on a vent then burns.
  • Walls block arrows, bolts and spells. Pillars and crates don't.
  • Two floors: in the Reeve's House the upper floor lies right over the ground floor, joined by one stair. Nobody sees, shoots or strikes through a floor, but from upstairs you can shoot down the stairwell. The switch on the left (or F) shows the upper floor or the ground floor; pieces on the other floor show as faint nameplates marked ▲ or ▼.

Foes

  • Cutters rush the weakest hero in reach. Archers keep their distance and shoot from up to 4 squares. Shamans mend hurt goblins and hex from range 3.
  • Wargs run 3 squares and hunt as a pack: +1 die on a hero another warg stands beside.
  • Grubnash fills a 2×2 block, cleaves every hero beside him, can't be shoved, and kicks the fallen out of his way. Twice he blows his war horn for two more cutters.
  • Grubnash Smash: he lowers his head and the path ahead of him is marked in red. At the start of his next turn he charges down it: anyone in the way is thrown aside, knocked Prone and takes 1d10. If he hits a wall, he falls Prone. Get out of the marked path.
  • When Grubnash falls, his court loses heart and flees, and the battle is won.
